About the analysis

How LineUp JD learns

What is collected, how it is calculated, and what the results mean.

1 · Collected during matches

Coach actions and team outcomes

Lineup & position

Who entered, who left, each named position, and the exact game time of every change.

Outcomes

Score for, score against, final result, margin, and win rate.

Context

Match format and opponent today; kickoff time and weather can be added later.

Not required: passes, assists, tackles, blocks, or tactical player actions.

2 · Reconstructed locally

The event timeline becomes lineup stints

00:00Starting lineup11:24Substitution18:06Goal for

Every uninterrupted lineup is assigned its duration, players, positions, and goals for and against. Editing the timeline recalculates these stints automatically.

Formation stint averages

Goals for and against are totaled across exact stints, then divided by total formation minutes. Longer stints receive proportionally more weight.

Bayesian small-data smoothing

With only a few games, scoring rates are pulled toward the team's overall rate. The pull is strongest for formations with the fewest minutes and fades as evidence grows.

Interpretation boundary

Smoothing reduces unstable early extremes but cannot manufacture evidence or prove that a formation caused the result.

3 · Calculated from your data

Hierarchical Bayesian scoring models

InputLineup stints + duration
→
Rate modelsScore for + score against
→
OutputsWin rate, margin, strongest factors

Team-centered Gamma–Poisson models estimate score for and score against with tracked time as exposure. A Beta–Binomial model estimates win rate. This lightweight empirical-Bayes approach partially pools small samples toward the team average, and margin is derived from the two scoring rates. Every recording of at least 10 minutes is treated as a match automatically; no close-out action is required. Reports use their average recorded duration for per-match values. Player attempts may still be logged in the match timeline, but they do not drive reports.

4 · Interpreted cautiously

Association, uncertainty, and readiness

Posterior estimate

The model's best current estimate of the factor's effect.

Credible range

The plausible range around that estimate. Wider ranges mean more uncertainty.

Report unlocks

Detailed reports wait for enough matches, outcomes, variation, and position minutes.

Important: LineUp JD identifies modeled associations, not proof that a player caused a result. It recommends arrangements, not player actions.
