About the analysis

How CoachJD learns

What is collected, how it is calculated, and what the results mean.

1 · Collected during matches

Coach actions and team outcomes

Lineup & position

Who entered, who left, each named position, and the exact game time of every change.

Outcomes

Goals for and against, final result and margin, plus optional attempts for and against.

Context

Match format and opponent today; kickoff time and weather can be added later.

Not required: passes, assists, tackles, blocks, or tactical player actions.

2 · Reconstructed locally

The event timeline becomes lineup stints

00:00Starting lineup11:24Substitution18:06Goal for

Every uninterrupted lineup is assigned its duration, players, positions, goals, and attempts. Editing the timeline recalculates these stints automatically.

Formation stint averages

Formation goals and attempts are totaled across exact stints, then divided by total formation minutes. Longer stints receive proportionally more weight.

Bayesian small-data smoothing

With only a few games, formation rates are pulled toward the team's overall rate. The pull is strongest for formations with the fewest minutes and fades as evidence grows.

Interpretation boundary

Smoothing reduces unstable early extremes but cannot manufacture evidence or prove that a formation caused the result.

3 · Calculated by the analysis server

Bayesian hierarchical Poisson models

InputLineup stints + duration
→
Rate modelsGoals and attempts, for + against
→
OutputsWin rate, margin, strongest factors

Goals for and against are estimated separately. Small samples are partially pooled toward the team average, reducing extreme early rankings. Win rate and margin are then derived from those rates. Attempts use parallel models only when the coach records them consistently.

4 · Interpreted cautiously

Association, uncertainty, and readiness

Posterior estimate

The model's best current estimate of the factor's effect.

Credible range

The plausible range around that estimate. Wider ranges mean more uncertainty.

Report unlocks

Detailed reports wait for enough matches, outcomes, variation, and position minutes.

Important: CoachJD identifies modeled associations, not proof that a player caused a result. It recommends arrangements, not player actions.
