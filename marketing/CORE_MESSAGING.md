# Core Marketing Message

## Main promise

> **Every game tracked reveals more about your team.**

The app gives coaches an immediately useful digital clipboard for managing who plays, where, and when. As more games are tracked, it gradually reveals reliable patterns about playing time, positions, combinations, fatigue, conditions, and team performance.

## Player strength profiles

Each tracked game helps build a **strength profile** for every player. The profile should highlight where and how that player contributes most effectively, including:

- Positions and roles where the player performs well
- Teammate combinations that bring out their strengths
- Game situations in which they have a positive impact
- Positive trends developing across the season
- Demonstrated versatility, consistency, and growth

Suggested message:

> **Every game builds a clearer picture of each player's strengths.**

The profile is not a permanent label or a judgment of a player's potential. It should evolve as the player develops and as more reliable data becomes available.

## Parent and public view

The parent-facing or public profile should be **positive and development-focused**. It may show strengths, progress, accomplishments, participation, and encouraging trends.

It should not publicly show:

- Negative impact scores or labels
- Rankings that shame or compare children
- Weakness scores
- Predictions that limit a player's perceived potential
- Small-sample conclusions presented as facts

Sensitive analysis intended to help with coaching decisions should remain private to authorized coaches. Even in the private view, results should describe observed situations rather than blame a player.

Prefer language such as:

> **This player has shown strong results in these roles and combinations.**

Avoid language such as:

> **This player makes the team worse.**

## Progressive discovery

Each completed game should deliver an immediate summary while moving the team closer to deeper insights. The app can preview upcoming discoveries with honest, data-based progress messages:

> **Track one more game to begin exploring player-combination insights.**

> **This trend is developing. More games will improve confidence.**

Insights should unlock when enough information exists to make them useful, not simply to manufacture engagement.

## Supporting lines

- **Track the game. Discover the team.**
- **See every player's strengths more clearly.**
- **Live decisions. Season-long insights.**
- **The more you track, the more your team reveals.**
- **Know who to play, where, when, and with whom.**
- **Turn every game into a better understanding of your players.**
